Your Privacy is Sacred: Positron is designed to provide a calm, peaceful, and private sanctuary for your daily reflections, gratitude journaling, mood tracking, and spiritual verses. We treat your personal thoughts with the utmost respect and confidential security.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Positron ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and web application.

By using Positron, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum information required to deliver the app's meditative and journaling features:

  • Journal & Reflection Content: Personal journal entries, gratitude notes, affirmations, prayer reflections, and mood ratings that you voluntarily write and save.
  • Account Information (Optional): If you choose to sign in with Google or email via Firebase Authentication, we receive your basic profile information (such as your name, email address, and profile photo) to sync your journal across your devices.
  • Preferences & App Settings: Soundscapes, ambient volume preferences, chosen voice settings, theme preferences (light/dark mode), and selected sacred tradition/perspective.
  • Device & Usage Data: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic information (such as app version, OS platform, and crash reports) strictly used to fix bugs and ensure stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Allow you to create, view, edit, search, and securely back up your personal journal entries.
  • Provide personalized spiritual verses, calming audio readings, and guided breathing exercises.
  • Track your personal mood journey and emotional patterns over time for your self-reflection.
  • Synchronize your journal entries across sessions when authenticated.

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal diary entries or identifiable data with third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

4. Third-Party Services & Technologies

Positron utilizes standard, trusted cloud infrastructure providers to provide core functionality:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore Database): Used for secure user authentication and cloud synchronization of user notes. Governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Used for optional secondary database persistence and synchronization.
  • Google Gemini AI (Server-Side Proxy): Used optionally to provide personalized spiritual reflections and empathetic contextual guidance based on your selected theme. Inputs are processed ephemerally and are never used to train public AI models.
  • Web Speech / Text-to-Speech Engine: Used locally on your device or via browser speech synthesis to read sacred verses aloud in soothing tones. Audio synthesis occurs directly on your client device.

5. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/HTTPS in transit and encrypted database storage at rest) to safeguard your reflections. Access to your entries is protected by strict user-level authorization rules so that only you can access your private diary entries.

6. User Control & Data Deletion

You maintain complete ownership of your personal data:

  • Edit or Delete Entries: You can delete individual journal entries or mood logs directly within the app at any time.
  • Complete Account Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated journal entries by contacting us at the email below. All data will be permanently wiped from our databases within 7 business days.
  • Offline & Local Usage: You can use the app anonymously or offline; your entries will remain stored locally on your device in your browser's local storage.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us and we will promptly delete the data.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
